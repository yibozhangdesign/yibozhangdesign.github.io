01 — ABOUT

A little about me

Research at the intersection of people, technology, and nature.

I am a researcher and designer in Visual Communication Design at Yonsei University. My work brings human-centered research together with speculative and practical design.

Across conversational AI, cultural experience, and technology for ageing, I ask how interactive systems can make complex relationships easier to understand and more responsible to shape. I build prototypes, develop design frameworks, and study how people experience them in everyday contexts.
